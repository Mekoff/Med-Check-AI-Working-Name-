## 1. Project Overview

**Project Name:** Med-Check AI (working title)
AI-Driven Pharmacovigilance Platform

**Project Type:** Digital Health / MedTech MVP Development

**Classification:** Internationally Scalable, Innovation-Led

---

## 2. Vision Statement

To eliminate preventable adverse drug events (ADEs) globally by providing a
mobile-first, AI-driven pharmacovigilance tool that empowers patients,
caregivers, and clinicians — regardless of geography or healthcare setting —
to make safer, more informed medication decisions.

---

## 3. Problem Statement

Adverse drug events represent one of the most preventable causes of patient
harm worldwide. Patients managing multiple medications — particularly those
navigating unfamiliar healthcare systems, language barriers, or limited access
to clinical support — face disproportionate risk. Existing tools are fragmented,
clinician-facing, and inaccessible to the populations that need them most.
Med-Check AI addresses this gap directly.

---

## 4. Core Objective

Develop a cross-platform MVP capable of:

- Scanning medication packaging and labels via Optical Character Recognition (OCR)
- Identifying drug–drug and drug–substance interactions in real time
- Surfacing patient-friendly side effect summaries in accessible language
- Integrating with established pharmacological databases (e.g. RxNav API)
- Operating across diverse healthcare contexts, including international markets

---

## 5. Strategic Alignment

### Global Health Innovation

Med-Check AI is designed with global scalability at its core. The platform's
architecture supports multilingual deployment and cross-border regulatory
adaptability, positioning it for adoption across NHS, EU, and international
health ecosystems.

---

## 6. Scope

### In-Scope

- OCR-based medication label scanning (mobile camera and video input)
- RxNav API integration for drug interaction lookups
- Drug–drug and drug–substance interaction flagging
- Patient-friendly, plain-language side effect summaries
- Cross-platform mobile MVP (iOS & Android)
- GDPR, MHRA, and NHS Digital compliance framework
- Alpha and beta testing with patient and caregiver cohorts

### Out-of-Scope

- Direct prescribing or prescription generation
- Diagnosis of new or existing medical conditions
- Pediatric-specific dosing calculations
- Real-time clinician–patient teleconsultation
- Electronic Health Record (EHR) system integration (post-MVP)

## 8. High-Level Deliverables

1. Functional cross-platform mobile MVP (iOS & Android)
2. OCR scanning module with medication recognition capability
3. Drug interaction engine integrated with RxNav API
4. Patient-facing side effect summary interface
5. Compliance documentation (GDPR, MHRA, NHS Digital)
6. Alpha/Beta testing report with user feedback analysis
7. MVP launch-ready build with deployment pipeline

---

## 9. Assumptions & Constraints

### Assumptions

- RxNav API remains publicly accessible and maintains current data standards
- Regulatory guidance from MHRA and NHS Digital remains stable during development
- Beta users (patients/caregivers) can be recruited through existing clinical networks

### Constraints

- MVP scope is bounded to adult medication management (no pediatric dosing)
- Platform does not replace clinical judgement or professional medical advice
- Data residency requirements must comply with UK GDPR throughout
