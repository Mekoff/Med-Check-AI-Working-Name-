Med-Check AI 
## 📈 Business Case (The Strategic Justification)

### 📊 Market Opportunity
Adverse Drug Events (ADEs) account for roughly **5–10% of hospital admissions** in the United Kingdom. A digital health tool capable of reducing this rate by even 1% offers massive, measurable cost savings for the NHS and private healthcare insurers.

---

### ⚠️ Problem Statement
Existing clinical decision support systems are notoriously cumbersome and exclusively designed for healthcare practitioners rather than patients. Furthermore, there is a critical market gap regarding consumer-facing **"Drug-to-Substance"** interaction scanning (e.g., cross-referencing *Ibuprofen* with *Alcohol* or herbal supplements) within current mobile health applications.

---

### 💰 Financial & Operational Model

#### 🪙 Revenue Streams
* **B2B Enterprise Licensing:** Subscriptions for community pharmacies, private health insurers, and corporate wellness platforms.
* **B2C Freemium Model:** Free core scanning features for patients, with premium tiers for longitudinal medication tracking, family accounts, and exportable health reports.

#### 📉 Cost Drivers
* **API Infrastructure:** Licensing fees for localized clinical drug databases (e.g., FDB, Medscape, or RxNav).
* **Compliance & Hosting:** Secure, regional cloud infrastructure meeting strict **GDPR** and **NHS Digital** data protection standards.
